FWIK: Multi-Waypoint IK Selection via Lookahead Graph Search

Jinglan Cao, Guohui Zhong, Chengwei Zhang

arXiv preprint, 2026

FWIK selects inverse-kinematics solutions by considering a short window of future waypoints and screening likely self-collisions before downstream motion planning.

arXiv / PDF

Insights behind FWIK
  1. Short-horizon lookahead. Many manipulation tasks expose a short sequence of future goals, making limited lookahead both possible and useful.
  2. Redundancy as a resource. A manipulator’s kinematic redundancy can be spent preparing its configuration for the next action.
  3. Bounded horizons. The value of future information decays with distance, so a small window can capture much of the benefit of full-sequence optimization at lower cost.
  4. Reachability is not continuity. A nearby IK solution may still require detours, reversals, or collision-prone transitions, while a slightly farther solution can yield smoother and faster motion.
